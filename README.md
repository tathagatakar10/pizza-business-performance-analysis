# 🍕 Pizza Business Performance Analysis

> **An end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed in Power BI to analyze financial performance, customer behavior, operational efficiency, workforce metrics, and branch performance for a fictional multi-location pizza restaurant business.**

> ![Executive Dashboard](Dashboard%20Preview/01%20Executive%20Dashboard.png)

---

# Executive Summary

Modern businesses generate large volumes of operational data every day. However, data alone rarely leads to better decisions. The real challenge is transforming that information into meaningful insights that help management understand performance, identify opportunities, and take informed action.

This project demonstrates how Business Intelligence can support that process. Using Power BI, I developed an interactive reporting solution for a fictional multi-location pizza restaurant business. The project combines financial, operational, customer, workforce, and branch data into a single reporting experience that enables decision-makers to monitor business performance from multiple perspectives.

Rather than focusing solely on dashboard design, the project emphasizes business thinking. Each analysis was developed to answer practical business questions, support evidence-based decision-making, and encourage further investigation where additional data would strengthen strategic decisions.

---

# Overview

Pizza Business Performance Analysis is an end-to-end Business Intelligence portfolio project developed to demonstrate how data can be transformed into meaningful business insights.

The project follows the complete Business Intelligence workflow, beginning with data preparation and progressing through data modeling, DAX development, dashboard design, interactive reporting, and business analysis. Instead of presenting isolated visualizations, the dashboards are designed to work together, providing a connected view of business performance across finance, customers, operations, employees, customer support, and branch performance.

The business scenario is fictional, allowing the project to simulate realistic business challenges while focusing on practical analytical techniques commonly used in business reporting environments.

---

# Business Problem

Restaurant businesses operate across multiple functions, including finance, sales, operations, customer service, workforce management, and branch operations. While these functions generate significant amounts of data, decision-makers often struggle to view business performance from a single, integrated perspective.

When information is distributed across multiple reports or systems, identifying performance trends, understanding customer behavior, evaluating operational efficiency, and prioritizing business decisions becomes increasingly difficult.

The objective of this project is to address that challenge by consolidating business information into an interactive reporting solution that enables management to monitor performance, identify trends, and explore opportunities using data.

---

# Business Objectives

The project was designed around a set of practical business objectives commonly faced by restaurant businesses.

The solution aims to:

- Monitor overall business performance using consolidated executive KPIs.
- Evaluate financial performance across revenue, cost, profitability, and sales trends.
- Understand customer behavior through segmentation and purchasing patterns.
- Measure customer support performance and service quality.
- Analyze workforce composition and employee-related metrics.
- Compare branch performance using operational and financial indicators.
- Present business insights that support informed decision-making.

---

# Solution Overview

The solution was developed as a multi-page interactive Power BI report that brings together data from several business functions into a single analytical experience.

The report consists of six business-focused dashboards supported by interactive navigation, dynamic filtering, custom tooltips, and KPI reporting. Each dashboard addresses a specific business area while remaining connected through a common data model, allowing users to move between strategic and operational perspectives without losing analytical context.

Rather than functioning as independent reports, the dashboards collectively tell the story of how different parts of the business contribute to overall performance.

# Dashboard Overview

The report is organized into six dashboards, each designed to answer a different set of business questions while contributing to a broader understanding of organizational performance.

### Executive Dashboard

![Executive Dashboard](Dashboard%20Preview/01%20Executive%20Dashboard.png)


The Executive Dashboard provides a consolidated view of overall business performance, allowing decision-makers to monitor key operational and financial metrics from a single page. The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How is the business performing overall?
- Which products and branches contribute the most revenue?
- Are key financial indicators improving over time?
- Which business areas require further investigation?

- How are revenue, cost, and profit changing over time?

# Analytical Approach

This project was designed to move beyond descriptive reporting by encouraging analytical thinking throughout the decision-making process. While the dashboards present historical business performance, the objective is not simply to report what happened but to provide a foundation for understanding why it happened and what should be investigated next.

Rather than relying on individual metrics, the analysis considers multiple business indicators together before drawing conclusions. Revenue, profitability, customer behavior, operational performance, workforce metrics, and investment efficiency are evaluated collectively to provide a more balanced view of business performance.

Where the available data supports a clear observation, those findings are presented directly. Where additional business context would strengthen decision-making, the analysis highlights opportunities for further investigation instead of making assumptions. This approach reflects how business decisions are typically made in practice, where data supports judgment rather than replacing it.

The insights presented throughout this project are therefore intended to support evidence-based discussions and identify areas where additional analysis could provide greater confidence before strategic decisions are made.

# Business Insights & Management Considerations

The following observations illustrate how the dashboards can be used to identify business opportunities and guide further analysis. These insights are intended to demonstrate an analytical approach rather than provide definitive business conclusions.

---

## 1. Revenue Seasonality & Business Planning

### Observation

Revenue trends indicate that business performance varies throughout the year, with stronger performance during some periods than others.

### Business Significance

Understanding seasonal demand helps management allocate resources more effectively. Staffing, inventory planning, marketing campaigns, and promotional activities can all be aligned with expected business activity to improve operational efficiency.

### Additional Analysis Required

Before making strategic decisions, it would be beneficial to examine:

- Product category performance by season
- Customer purchasing behaviour
- Promotional activity
- Branch-level performance
- Holiday and event calendars
- Regional demand patterns

### Potential Business Experiments

- Evaluate seasonal marketing campaigns.
- Test limited-time menu offerings during slower periods.
- Adjust staffing levels based on expected demand.
- Compare promotional performance across different seasons.

---

## 2. Product Portfolio & Bundle Optimization

### Observation

Individual orders contribute a larger share of revenue than bundled offerings, suggesting an opportunity to better understand customer purchasing preferences.

### Business Significance

Bundle performance can influence both customer value and overall profitability. Improving bundle adoption may increase average order value while creating additional value for customers.

### Additional Analysis Required

Future analysis should explore:

- Average basket value
- Bundle conversion rates
- Frequently purchased product combinations
- Pricing strategy
- Profit margins
- Price elasticity

### Potential Business Experiments

- Test revised bundle pricing.
- Evaluate value-oriented meal combinations.
- Compare bundle performance before and after promotional campaigns.
- Measure changes in average order value following bundle redesign.

---

Customer segments contribute differently to business performance, with loyal and high-value customers generating a significant share of overall revenue.

### Business Significance

Not every customer contributes equally to long-term business growth. Understanding customer value helps organizations prioritize retention efforts and allocate marketing resources more effectively.

### Additional Analysis Required

Service quality is influenced by multiple factors beyond response time alone. Understanding both operational efficiency and customer satisfaction provides a more complete picture of customer experience.

### Additional Analysis Required

# Building the Solution

Transforming data into meaningful business insights requires more than creating dashboards. It involves preparing reliable data, designing a scalable data model, developing reusable business logic, and presenting information in a way that supports informed decision-making.

This project was built using an end-to-end Business Intelligence workflow, beginning with data preparation in Microsoft Excel and progressing through data modeling, DAX development, interactive dashboard design, and business analysis in Power BI. Each stage was designed to create a reporting solution that is both technically robust and focused on practical business use.

---

## Data Preparation

The source data required preparation before it could be used for analysis. Microsoft Excel was used to review the datasets, resolve data quality issues, and create missing business calculations that were not available in the original files.

The preparation process included validating data consistency, generating missing financial calculations, and creating additional business metrics that would later support reporting and analysis within Power BI. Completing these steps before importing the data helped establish a cleaner analytical foundation and reduced unnecessary complexity during model development.

Although this project focuses on reporting and business analysis, the quality of those insights depends on the quality of the underlying data. Preparing the data before modeling ensured that the dashboards were built on consistent and reliable information.

---

## Data Model

Once the data was prepared, the datasets were imported into Power BI and organized into a relational semantic model designed to support cross-functional business analysis.

The model connects financial, customer, employee, branch, product, marketing, fulfilment, and customer support data through structured relationships, allowing business metrics to remain consistent across all report pages. Supporting tables were also developed to enable time intelligence calculations, category analysis, and dashboard-specific reporting requirements.

The completed semantic model consists of:

| Component | Count |
|-----------|------:|
| Tables | 20 |
| Relationships | 29 |
| Measures | 192 |
| Calculated Columns | 23 |
| Calculated Tables | 3 |
| Dashboard Pages | 6 |
| Tooltip Pages | 2 |
| Total Report Pages | 8 |
| Bookmarks | 5 |
| Navigation Buttons | 7 |
| Slicers | 9 |
| Custom Tooltip Pages | 2 |

Together, these components form the analytical foundation that enables consistent reporting across every dashboard while supporting dynamic filtering, reusable calculations, and interactive exploration.

---

## Dashboard Development

With the semantic model in place, the focus shifted to designing an interactive reporting experience that would make business information accessible and actionable.

The report was developed as a collection of six interconnected dashboards, each representing a different business function while remaining part of a unified analytical solution. Rather than treating dashboards as isolated reports, the design encourages users to move naturally between executive summaries and detailed operational analysis.

Several interactive features were incorporated to improve usability and create a smoother reporting experience, including:

- Interactive navigation between dashboard pages
- Dynamic KPI cards with conditional formatting
- Time intelligence and Year-over-Year comparisons
- Interactive slicers for focused analysis
- Custom tooltip pages providing additional context
- Bookmarks for navigation and report interactions
- Consistent visual design across all dashboards

Design decisions throughout the report prioritize clarity over visual complexity. Colours, typography, spacing, and layout were selected to improve readability while maintaining a consistent user experience across the entire report.

---

## AI-Assisted Development

Artificial Intelligence was used as a supporting tool during selected stages of development rather than as a replacement for analytical work.

AI assisted with generating the initial structure for certain DAX calculations, creating the calendar table, and exploring ideas for branding and logo design. Every AI-generated output was reviewed, tested, and adapted before being incorporated into the final solution.

The development process remained iterative and hands-on. Business requirements were defined first, potential solutions were evaluated, DAX calculations were validated using Power BI, and outputs were refined until they aligned with the intended analytical objectives.

This approach allowed AI to accelerate parts of the development process while ensuring that the final business logic, analytical decisions, dashboard design, and project narrative remained the result of independent implementation and critical evaluation.
